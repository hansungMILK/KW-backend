# General Shorts Preset

Fallback preset for topics that do not match a dedicated subject preset.

- Prefer primary sources, official pages, direct announcements, or reputable reporting.
- Preserve source references for factual claims.
- If source confidence is weak, avoid definitive phrasing.
- Use a fast hook, short captions, clear narration, and one visual idea per scene.
- GPT-image-2 may render short Korean title/caption text in-frame.
- Do not force topic-specific source policy into this preset.
