# Shorts BGM Rules

Background music is part of the final MP4 composition, not a separate mock step.

## Source Policy

- Do not download or use random copyrighted YouTube Music tracks at runtime.
- Only use tracks that are royalty-free, directly licensed, or supplied by the project owner.
- Preserve track id, title, source, license, and attribution in the video output metadata.

## Mood Mapping

- `warm-storytelling`: admission, school, insurance, calm information, social explanation.
- `cinematic-tension`: war, history, international issues, incidents, mystery, shock.
- `fast-explainer`: tips, product explainers, how-to, comparison, review.
- `quirky-office`: work, psychology, relationships, office humor.
- `futuristic-tech`: AI tools, developer tools, automation, workflow products.
- `neutral-documentary`: default when the topic is unclear.

## Mixing

- Narration is primary.
- BGM must sit below narration.
- Default BGM volume should stay around 0.04-0.06 when narration is present.
- Avoid vocal music unless the content explicitly needs it and the license allows it.
- Prefer loopable instrumental beds with minimal melody. Information Shorts need tension and pace, not a song competing with TTS.
