# Education Admission Shorts Preset

This preset is the first high-quality template for Korean admission-information Shorts.

## Source Policy

- Primary sources: Ministry of Education, ADIGA/대입정보포털, KICE/한국교육과정평가원, KCUE/한국대학교육협의회, and official university admission pages.
- News and blogs are secondary context only.
- Dates, admission type names, score/rank claims, ratios, deadlines, and schedule claims require an official source.
- If no official source is available, mark the claim as "확인 필요" or soften it.

## Tone

- Audience: Korean students and parents.
- Style: fast, practical, clear, and direct.
- Avoid fearmongering.
- Avoid "무조건", "100%", "합격 보장", and other guaranteed-outcome wording.

## Visual Pattern

- Top title: short, bold, high contrast.
- Main caption: short punchline or key fact.
- Scene: classroom, calendar, counseling, score report, application checklist, university campus, graph metaphor.
- Source label: short, e.g. "기준: 대입정보포털".
